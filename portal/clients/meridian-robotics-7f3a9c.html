Autonomous Inspection Rover

A tracked, tele-operable rover that runs autonomous pipe-gallery inspection passes — capturing synced thermal + RGB imagery and flagging anomalies for human review.

Overview

Meridian needs to retire manual confined-space inspections. The rover carries a stabilised camera head through gallery runs, holds a steady crawl over uneven substrate, and produces a georeferenced defect report per pass — no operator in the space.

My role spans the mechanical drivetrain, the embedded control stack, and the sensor-fusion glue that turns raw frames into actionable flags. The plan below is the working agreement — scope, milestones, and what lands when.

Scope

✓ In scope

  • Tracked chassis + suspension for ≤ 25° grades
  • STM32 motor-control firmware, closed-loop speed
  • Stabilised 2-axis camera gimbal
  • Thermal + RGB capture, hardware-synced
  • On-board anomaly pre-flagging (edge model)
  • Tele-op fallback over 900 MHz link

— Out of scope (for now)

  • Cloud dashboard / long-term data store
  • Multi-rover fleet coordination
  • Intrinsically-safe (ATEX) certification
  • Robotic manipulation / sample collection
