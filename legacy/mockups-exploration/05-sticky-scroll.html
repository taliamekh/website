🏆 Best Hardware Hack · GenAI Genesis

Sleep Apnea Detector

🥈 2nd · QNX Challenge · uOttaHack 8

A wearable sleep apnea risk detection system tracking respiratory depth and rate in real time, streaming data to a mobile app and triggering automated alerts on anomaly detection.

Pipeline
  • Custom spring-loaded potentiometer housing translates chest expansion into voltage
  • SPI + MCP3008 ADC on Raspberry Pi 5 (QNX OS)
  • HTTP → Express/TS REST API → PostgreSQL on Railway
  • SwiftUI iOS app: live breaths/min + alerts
My Role
  • Conceptualized the design and functional requirements
  • Reverse-engineered a badge reel into a custom potentiometer housing in Fusion 360
  • Integrated the full circuit framework

// PROJECT 03

Water Filter & No-Flow System

🏆 1st · Engineering Without Borders

A 5-hour design challenge to remediate a 20,000-liter industrial fertilizer spill. Designed two subsurface columns: filtration treatment (Column A) and a no-flow barrier (Column B). Only team to successfully reduce both turbidity and electrical conductivity vs the original solution.

Results
  • Turbidity (NTU): 1.67 → 0.5
  • Absorbance: 0.724 → 0.009
  • Conductivity (EV): 4.4 → 2.0
Constraint
  • AI use was strictly prohibited — manual research only
  • No real-contaminant testing until final judging
