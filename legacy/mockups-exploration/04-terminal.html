feat(wearable): Sleep Apnea Detector — QNX + iOS 🥈 2nd · QNX challenge
uOttaHack 8
36 hr

summary

A wearable sleep apnea risk detection system tracking respiratory depth and rate in real time, streaming to a mobile app and triggering automated alerts on anomaly.

pipeline

  • chest-expansion potentiometer in custom spring-loaded housing → variable voltage
  • SPI + MCP3008 ADC on Raspberry Pi 5 (QNX OS) → digital signal
  • HTTP → Express/TypeScript REST API → PostgreSQL on Railway
  • SwiftUI iOS app shows live breaths/min, depth classification, warning alerts

my contribution

  • conceptualized the design and functional requirements
  • reverse-engineered a badge reel into a custom potentiometer housing in Fusion 360
  • integrated the full circuit framework for hardware communication
