Robotics · AI Agents · 🏆 Best Hardware Hack — GenAI Genesis

DeskClaw —
giving AI a body.

An autonomous robot assistant designed to bridge the gap between digital intelligence and physical labor — a real-time collaborator that observes your workspace and helps when your hands are tied.

The Problem

Traditional AI agents are confined to screens. DeskClaw provides a functional body for engineers and makers in the workshop — vocalize design challenges, bounce ideas, get a second opinion on a mechanical problem, all while the robot observes the workspace.

The Build

Body: Tracked rover chassis driven by Raspberry Pi 5 + L289N motor driver. Arm: Fully 3D-printed 6-axis arm with Arduino servo control and inverse kinematics. Brain: Gemini 2.5 Flash for scene reasoning, Whisper ASR for voice, ElevenLabs for speech. Vision: Dual-camera YOLO11 for real-time object detection.

2 awards
Best Hardware Hack & Overall Finalist · GenAI Genesis

My Contribution

Conceived the project. Designed and 3D-printed every physical component. Planned and soldered all circuitry. Diagnosed and fixed motor power distribution failure hours before the deadline. Wrote inverse-kinematics arm firmware in C++ on Arduino during the hackathon window.

02
Wearable · Embedded · 🥈 2nd — QNX Challenge · uOttaHack 8

A wearable that watches you breathe.

A sleep apnea risk detection system tracking respiratory depth and rate in real time, streaming to a mobile app and triggering automated alerts on anomaly.

How It Works

A custom spring-loaded housing holds a potentiometer that translates chest expansion into variable voltage. Voltage is read digitally via SPI and an MCP3008 ADC on Raspberry Pi 5 running QNX OS. The data pipeline runs HTTP → Express/TypeScript REST API → PostgreSQL on Railway. An iOS app in SwiftUI displays live breaths/min, depth classification, and warning alerts.

My Contribution

Conceptualized the original design and functional requirements. Reverse engineered a badge reel mechanism into a custom potentiometer housing using Fusion 360. Developed and integrated the custom circuit framework for seamless hardware communication.

Sustainability · Materials Science · 🏆 1st — EWB Sustainable Design

A 5-hour fix for a 20,000-litre spill.

Two subsurface columns designed and built under time pressure to remediate an industrial fertilizer spill near a Significant Groundwater Recharge Zone.

The Design

Column A — filtration: 9-layer system from drainage gravel through sand, biochar, GAC, ion-exchange resin, green sand, and cotton, each chosen for a specific contaminant pathway. Column B — flow prevention: Gravel + sand + clay barrier with sponge and hydrophobic-cloth failsafes.

Result

Turbidity dropped from 1.67 NTU → 0.5. Absorbance from 0.724 → 0.009. Conductivity from 4.4 EV → 2.0.

Only team
to reduce both turbidity AND electrical conductivity vs the original solution

The Constraint

AI usage was strictly prohibited. No way to test against the actual contaminant solution until final judging — the design relied entirely on theoretical material interactions.
